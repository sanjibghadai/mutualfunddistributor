About

A planning-first approach to your money

Every financial decision should serve a goal you actually care about — a child's education, a comfortable retirement, a home, or simply peace of mind. Sanjib works with clients to map those goals first, then builds an investment and insurance plan to match.

As an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor and Insurance Consultant, the focus stays on long-term discipline over short-term speculation — SIPs, systematic transfers and withdrawals, and the right cover, reviewed regularly as life changes.
